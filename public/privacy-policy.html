Privacy Policy

Plain-language details about how SpendVeil keeps expense information private.

SpendVeil is provided by Ash Moobed. This policy explains how the current iPhone app handles information.

Information we collect

The current app does not create an account, use advertising or analytics SDKs, connect to banks, or send expense records, receipt images, or recognized receipt text to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Expenses, recurring rules, categories, settings, receipt images, and recognized text are stored locally inside the app’s iOS container. Receipt recognition is performed on-device using Apple frameworks.

Offline Ask SpendVeil

Ask SpendVeil recognizes a limited set of expense questions and calculates answers from local records without a language model or network request. Questions and calculated answers are temporary screen state and are not added to the app database. The feature cannot modify, delete, export, or share a record.

Optional Veil Assist

On compatible Apple Intelligence iPhones, you may turn on Veil Assist in Settings. It uses Apple’s on-device System Language Model with Apple’s default safety guardrails; SpendVeil does not provide a network or third-party AI fallback. The feature may process a search request, recognized receipt text, or precomputed report facts on the device. It is off by default, has no action tools, and cannot save, delete, export, share, change a budget, or make financial decisions. AI drafts can be wrong and must be reviewed by you.

Camera, photos, and files

The app accesses the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt. You may also choose a receipt from Photos or Files using Apple’s system pickers. Selected content is used to create the expense record you request.

Free edition

Every SpendVeil feature is included at no charge. The app has no in-app purchase, subscription, advertising, donation link, or external payment flow.

Exports and sharing

When you deliberately export or share a report, iOS sends the chosen file to the destination you select. That destination’s privacy practices apply after the transfer.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting an expense removes its attached receipt image. Settings also lets you remove all receipt images while keeping expense details and recognized text.

Children

The app is a general financial organization tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

If app behavior changes—such as adding cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or third-party services—this policy will be updated before or when that change is released.
