Support

How can we help?

SpendVeil keeps records on your iPhone, so support starts with clear, privacy-safe troubleshooting.

Contact

Email ashkijiji@gmail.com. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not attach financial records or receipts unless necessary.

Purchasing or restoring Pro

Open Settings and choose Unlock Pro or Restore Purchases. SpendVeil Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. If Apple reports a billing problem, review Media & Purchases in iPhone Settings.

Receipt scanning

Receipt scanning is a Pro feature. Use a flat, well-lit receipt and review detected details before saving. Manual entry remains free if camera access is denied.

Ask SpendVeil on any supported iPhone

Open Reports and type a question in Ask SpendVeil. It works offline on iPhone 11 and every iPhone supported by the app; Apple Intelligence is not required. Ask about totals, counts, categories, largest expenses, averages, tax-labelled expenses, budgets, or month/year comparisons. You may include a merchant, family member, currency, or amount threshold. Unsupported questions receive examples instead of a guessed answer.

Veil Assist

Veil Assist is an optional Pro feature on compatible Apple Intelligence iPhones running iOS 26 or later. Enable it in Settings. It uses Apple’s on-device model and has no network AI fallback. It prepares drafts only; review all suggestions and rely on the exact records, charts, and totals. If the device or model is unavailable, standard search, receipt OCR, categorization, reports, and recurring expenses continue to work.

Recurring expenses

Recurring expenses are free. Open Recurring to create weekly, monthly, or yearly rules, then edit, pause, or delete them at any time.

Finding a receipt

Open Expenses and search for a merchant, note, category, payer, tax label, or words recognized from a receipt already saved on the device.

Removing receipt images

Open Settings and choose Remove all receipt images. Amounts, categories, notes, and recognized searchable text remain.

Exporting

Exports are a Pro feature. Open Reports, choose a period and filters, then use Export. CSV files open in Google Sheets; .xls files are Excel-compatible.
